# Evaluation Criteria

## Scoring Scale

| Score | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Major failure |
| 2 | Weak; substantial correction required |
| 3 | Acceptable with notable limitations |
| 4 | Good with minor issues |
| 5 | Excellent against defined expectation |

## Dimensions

### 1. Factual Accuracy
Were supplied facts preserved? Look for changes in meaning, added scope, incorrect claims, or stronger statements than the source supports.

### 2. Hallucination Control
Were unsupported claims avoided? Check metrics, percentages, tools, technologies, achievements, responsibilities, ownership, and business impact.

### 3. Clarity
Is the output understandable and direct?

### 4. Conciseness
Is unnecessary wording, repetition, and filler avoided?

### 5. Instruction Following
Were the applicable prompt constraints followed, including bullet count, format, length guidance, and first-person restrictions?

### 6. Professional Quality
Is the wording useful as resume content without rewarding invented claims?

### 7. Ambiguity Handling
Was guessing avoided when the input was vague?

## Important Scoring Rule

Score the actual output against the defined criterion. Do not score against what the model probably meant.
